Analyser

Manager breakdowns, trade analysis and scoring stats — pulled live from the FPL Draft API.

How to use it
1
Enter your league ID and generate your bookmarklet
Type your FPL Draft league ID below and click Generate. A personal bookmarklet link will appear, pre-loaded with your league.
2
Drag the link to your bookmarks bar
Make sure your bookmarks bar is visible (Ctrl+Shift+B on most browsers), then drag the green link straight onto it. Or use the manual copy instructions below it.
3
Go to draft.premierleague.com while logged in
The analyser needs your FPL session to fetch data. Make sure you are logged in on the FPL Draft site in the same browser.
4
Click your bookmark — the analyser opens instantly
A full-screen panel will appear showing manager breakdowns and trade analysis. It takes 30–60 seconds to load all the data.

Generate your bookmarklet

Drag the link below onto your bookmarks bar. If you cannot see your bookmarks bar, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) to show it.
FPL Draft Analyser
Cannot drag it? Click Copy, then right-click your bookmarks bar, choose "Add bookmark", paste the copied code as the URL, and name it "FPL Draft Analyser".
What the analyser shows
📊
Points by position
See how many points each manager earned from GKP, DEF, MID and FWD — started XI only.
🪑
Started vs benched
Compare how many points were left on the bench vs the active XI across the season.
🎯
Scoring categories
Goals, assists, clean sheets, bonus, saves, defensive contributions, deductions and minutes — all broken down per manager.
🔄
Trade analysis
Every trade with total pts and started pts per player since the trade gameweek, with a winner on each deal.
🛡️
Defensive contributions
DC points using your league rule: DEF 10+ DC = 2pts, MID/FWD 12+ DC = 2pts. GKPs excluded.
🏆
Live standings
Managers sorted by current league rank with season totals on every card.
